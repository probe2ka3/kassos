Who is behind kassos

kassos is a personal humanitarian initiative by Rexhep Kllokoqi. My pledge is simple and public: 100% of what arrives at this address is redistributed as aid to the most vulnerable. The causes may evolve over time; the rule never will.

This is a personal charitable initiative, not a registered tax-exempt charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. In return, you get full on-chain transparency — you can follow every single coin.

Questions

Where does the money go?
To humanitarian aid for people in need. The focus may change with the most urgent needs, but 100% is always redistributed.
How can I verify donations?
Open the address on the Kaspa explorer — every incoming and outgoing transaction is public and time-stamped.
Why Kaspa and not a bank?
Kaspa is borderless, settles in seconds, and costs a fraction of a cent — so aid reaches people quickly and almost nothing is lost to fees.
What is Kaspa (KAS)?
Kaspa is a fast, open cryptocurrency. You can buy KAS on most major exchanges and send it from any Kaspa wallet.
